    Privacy & security

    Your keystrokes are
    nobody's business.

    Everything Crispy sends — every key press, every click, every copy — is encrypted before it leaves your computer, and can only be read by the computers you've paired.

    Noise_XX_25519_ChaChaPoly_BLAKE2s

    Encrypted, end to end.

    Built on the Noise protocol: a XX handshake with Curve25519 keys, ChaCha20‑Poly1305 encryption, and BLAKE2s hashing.

    Only computers you trust.

    A computer has to be paired with a six‑digit code before it can connect. You can create a fresh identity at any time to start over.

    No accounts. No cloud.

    Crispy talks directly between your computers on your local network. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.

    Setup

    Up and running in a minute.

    1. 1

      Open the disk image.

      Double-click Crispy_1.0.0_universal.dmg, then drag Crispy into your Applications folder.

    2. 2

      Open Crispy.

      If macOS says it can't verify the app, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

    3. 3

      Allow Accessibility.

      Crispy needs Accessibility permission to share your keyboard and mouse. Turn it on in Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

    4. 4

      Pair and arrange.

      Enter the six-digit code from your other computer, then drag your screens into place in Arrangement.
